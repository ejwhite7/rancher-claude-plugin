# Rancher

![Rancher](assets/rancher-icon.png)

Assess potential AI-data licensing opportunities from your employee count, company age, retained history and business tools. Explore potential AI uses for your records, review a qualified illustrative US benchmark, and prepare a reusable licensing discussion brief.

## Install privately

In Claude, open **Customize > Plugins > Add > Upload plugin** and select the Claude ZIP. Connect Rancher from the plugin's **Connectors** tab with **No sign in**. For Claude Code, load the extracted folder with `claude --plugin-dir ./rancher-claude`. Enable the connector before requesting an assessment.

Try: "Assess the opportunity for a US business with 100 employees, a company age of 10 years, 10 retained years, and Slack, GitHub and Zendesk. We have licensing authority and access to the records."

## Data and safeguards

Claude sends only the metadata you explicitly provide to https://rancher-production.up.railway.app/mcp, hosted on Railway. Do not upload records, credentials, employee details or chat logs. No connected business accounts, payments or automatic leads are part of this plugin. The application does not save assessment inputs or log input bodies; hosting providers and Claude may process request metadata under their own policies.

Company age is not retained history. Dollar figures are illustrative marketing benchmarks, not valuations, guaranteed offers or verified buyer demand. Actual licensing proceeds may be zero. Permission or access blockers suppress dollar figures. Tool names represent potential data sources, not connected integrations.

You may take your brief away independently. The optional https://www.gorancher.com/#contact link opens a separate inquiry without assessment data in the URL. No records or contact details are automatically sent.
