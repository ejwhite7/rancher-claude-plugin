# Business-data opportunity assessment

Use for an explicit request to assess business-record licensing opportunity or prepare a quote-discussion brief. Do not activate for unrelated pricing, personal-data sales, financial investments or tool administration.

1. Collect current employee count, company age in complete years and selected business tools. Use get_data_catalog to map names or aliases to canonical tool IDs when needed. Never request a name, email, phone, records, credentials or chat history.
2. Call assess_data_opportunity with explicit metadata. Do not silently substitute company age for retainedHistoryYears or personal location for usBusiness. Unknown optional facts remain unknown. An incomplete but valid assessment can ask focused follow-up questions.
3. Explain the stack-specific record/use-case hypotheses and limitations. Buyer archetypes are not confirmed buyers. Selected tools are not connected integrations or proof of rights.
4. A returned dollar scenario is a public marketing benchmark, not a valuation, quote, minimum offer or earnings forecast. Actual proceeds may be zero. No tool-specific dollar multipliers or repeat-sale assumptions apply. Do not extrapolate outside coverage.
5. Review missing permissions, retention, quality and excluded records. Do not solicit PHI, financial identifiers, employee records, third-party code, secrets or personal data. De-identification does not eliminate risk or create rights.
6. If requested, call prepare_quote_brief using explicit metadata. The brief is generated independently, not saved or sent. A user may take the findings away without any referral.
7. An optional link to https://www.gorancher.com/#contact opens a separate partnership inquiry to discuss a quote. Never submit a lead, imply an instant quote or acceptance, force a redirect, or put assessment content in a link.

The complete assessment works as text even when an MCP Apps host cannot render the card.
